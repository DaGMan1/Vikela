You're almost ready
Vikela works automatically once you open Gmail. No sign-in required. Just three quick steps:
1
Pin Vikela to your toolbar
Click the puzzle piece icon in Chrome → find Vikela → click the pin 📌
2
Open Gmail and click the Vikela icon
The sidebar will open on the right side of your screen
3
Open any email
Vikela detects the sender automatically and shows their profile, trust score, and domain intel
🔒 100% Private: Vikela stores everything locally on your device. Your emails and contact data never leave your machine.
